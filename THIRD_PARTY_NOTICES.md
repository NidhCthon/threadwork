The Elysium theme ships two typefaces in `styles/fonts/`, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose full text is beside them:

- **Cinzel**, copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors (https://github.com/NDISCOVER/Cinzel). `OFL-Cinzel.txt`.
- **Cormorant Garamond**, copyright 2015 The Cormorant Project Authors (https://github.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant). `OFL-CormorantGaramond.txt`.

The files are the latin and latin-ext variable WOFF2 builds from Fontsource (`@fontsource-variable/cinzel` and `@fontsource-variable/cormorant-garamond`, 5.3.0), unmodified.
